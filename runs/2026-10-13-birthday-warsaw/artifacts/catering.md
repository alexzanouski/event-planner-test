## Summary

Recommended caterer: Frykasy y Ananasy, a Warsaw and Lodz finger-food caterer that delivers 7 days a week. The order is two ready-made cold sets: the standard set for 20 people and the vegetarian set for 20 people, 2018 PLN plus delivery. Drinks are bought by the host in a shop, which the venue allows. The food is a cold self-service buffet kept in the venue's bar fridge, so no serving staff are needed. Total catering cost is 524.15 EUR, about 26.21 EUR per guest for 20 guests. The 3 vegetarians are covered by a full vegetarian set, much more than they need.

## Details

### Venue constraints

- Events4U No.2 allows own food and alcohol, so an external caterer and own drinks are allowed (see venue sources).
- The venue page lists "bar, lodowka, stoly i podstawowe zaplecze" (bar, fridge, tables and basic facilities) as included. No kitchen is listed. The plan therefore uses only cold food that needs no heating, cooking or plating. The bar fridge keeps the dinner platters, leftovers and juices cold. Cool boxes are only a backup in case the fridge is too small for two sets plus drinks.
- Hot drinks (tea, coffee) depend on a kettle or hot-water source at the bar. A kettle is not confirmed (see Open issues).
- Bar service ("profesjonalna obsluga baru") is not included in the venue price. This plan does not need it. All food comes on the caterer's ready platters and all drinks are self-service at the bar. The host or a helper only moves platters between the fridge and the buffet table at the times in the serving plan. No serving or bar staff are budgeted.

### Selected caterer

Selected caterer: Frykasy y Ananasy (frykasyananasy.pl, phone 512 100 522 / 512 100 523). Reasons:

- It delivers in Warsaw on weekends, including Sunday, between 8:00 and 20:00, so delivery before the 16:00 start works.
- All food is cold finger food on ready platters. It needs no kitchen and no serving staff, which suits a venue with a bar and fridge.
- It sells a complete vegetarian set for 20 people. Ordering it next to the standard set covers the 3 vegetarians many times over and gives everyone a mixed buffet.
- Two sets for 2018 PLN are above the Sunday minimum order for Warsaw (1000-1250 PLN).
- About 473 EUR for food and delivery leaves most of the 2000 EUR budget for the venue and the photographer.

Orders must be placed at least 48 hours ahead, which means by 2026-10-23 for a 2026-10-25 delivery.

## Open issues

- Order deadline: order by 2026-10-23 (48 hours ahead) and ask for a 15:30-16:00 delivery to the venue on Sunday 2026-10-25. The venue must let the host in by 15:30 (see the venue setup-time open issue). If it cannot, ask for delivery at 16:00 and put the starters out as soon as they arrive.
- Delivery cost is shown only as a 29-59 PLN range and is set after entering the postcode. The plan uses the upper bound of 59 PLN. Confirm the Warsaw Sunday minimum (1000-1250 PLN) for the venue's postcode. The order of 2018 PLN is above it.
- Fridge: the bar fridge is confirmed by the venue page, but its size is not. Ask Events4U whether it holds the dinner platters from two sets plus juices. If not, bring cool boxes as a backup.
- Kettle: not confirmed. Ask Events4U. Without a kettle, bring one or leave out tea and coffee (saves 12.98 EUR).
- Drinks: Biedronka online currently shows all drinks in this plan as "Chwilowo niedostepny" (temporarily unavailable). That covers the water, both juices, Pepsi-Cola and Sprite as well as the tea and coffee. The host buys all drinks in a shop before the event, ideally on 2026-10-24. Shop prices may differ slightly from the online prices used here. The 0.50 PLN deposit is refunded when the empty bottles are returned.
- Staff: no serving or bar staff are planned or budgeted. The venue's paid bar service is not needed for a self-service buffet. Someone (host or a helper) should be named to move platters to and from the fridge at 16:45, 17:45 and 19:45.
- Plates, cutlery, napkins and cups are not stated as included in the sets. Ask the caterer. Otherwise the host buys disposable tableware (not costed here).
- Allergens: the pear tartlets contain nuts and the tahini dessert contains sesame. Label them for the children. Ask the caterer to confirm that the cheeses in the vegetarian set are made with vegetarian (non-animal) rennet.
- The birthday cake for 19:45-20:15 and alcohol are not in this plan. Add them if the user wants them; the venue allows own alcohol.

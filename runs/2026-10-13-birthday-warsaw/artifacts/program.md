## Summary

The party runs indoors at Events4U No.2 on 2026-10-25 from 16:00 to 22:00 local time (CET; clocks go back one hour that morning). The program has 9 slots: welcome, group photo, dinner, a 2-hour children's animation by Swiat Wrazen (1 animator, up to 10 children), the birthday cake with desserts, a guest quiz, dancing and a farewell. The only paid item is the animator: 550 PLN, about 125.32 EUR. Music and lighting for the rest of the evening use the venue's sound and lighting, which are included in the rental.

Fit with the venue (Events4U No.2):

- What the venue includes: sound and party lighting, a bar with a fridge, tables and basic facilities, cleaning after the party, and security. According to the venue page, staff/security are on site to help keep order ("Na miejscu obecna jest obsluga / ochrona, ktora pomaga zadbac o porzadek"). Bar service ("profesjonalna obsluga baru") is not part of the price.
- Space: the hall holds up to 50 people. With 20 guests plus the photographer there are 21 people, which the venue planner already counted.
- Light: the venue's party lighting is mood lighting, not photo lighting. The photographer brings studio lighting for group and portrait shots. During the cake slot (19:45-20:15) the program dims the venue lights, so the photographer should plan flash or studio light for that moment.
- Power: the venue page does not list power sockets or limits under its basic facilities. The host asks Events4U before the event for one free socket near the group-photo area, away from the children's play corner and the bar.
- Sound limits: none apply, because photography makes no noise.
- Coordination: the host is the photographer's main contact for the arrival time, access and where to leave equipment cases. The venue's on-site staff/security are there to keep order and are not a dedicated contact for the photographer.

Fit with the weather: the event is indoors, so the forecast does not affect the photographer. The forecast for 16:00-17:00 is dry and 10.8-11.6 °C, so the optional outdoor group photo at the entrance (program slot 16:30-16:45) is possible.

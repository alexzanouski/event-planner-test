## Summary

Recommended venue: Events4U No.2, a private party hall in Warsaw Praga-Poludnie for up to 50 people. Its published starting price is 1799 PLN (about 409.90 EUR). It allows guests to bring their own food and alcohol. According to the venue page, the price covers sound and lighting ("naglosnienie i swiatla"), a bar with a fridge and basic facilities ("bar, lodowka, stoly i podstawowe zaplecze"), cleaning after the party ("sprzatanie po imprezie") and security ("ochrona w cenie"). A cheaper hall in Wola (1300 PLN, about 296.20 EUR) is the backup if Events4U is not available on Sunday 2026-10-25.

Recommended venue: Events4U No.2. It is indoor and holds up to 50 people, well above the 20 guests, so G2 is covered. It costs about 409.90 EUR, about 20% of the 2000 EUR budget. That leaves room for catering and the photographer. The source page explicitly allows own food and alcohol, so the catering planner can use any caterer, including one with vegetarian options. The bar fridge can keep drinks and cold food cool. Sound and lighting, cleaning after the party and security are included in the price, so there are no extra costs for those. As a private, exclusive-use hall, it gives the up to 3 children a safe, closed space. The child-friendly program is up to the program planner.

Fallback space: none needed (indoor event).

## Open issues

- Events4U No.2: 1799 PLN is a starting price. Confirm the final price for Sunday 2026-10-25, 16:00-22:00, and whether setup and cleanup time adds to it. Get the street address, step-free access and parking details. None of these are on the source page.
- Events4U No.2: bar service ("profesjonalna obsluga baru") is not listed as included. If the catering plan needs serving staff, the catering planner has to arrange and budget for them.
- Events4U No.2: availability on 2026-10-25 is not confirmed. If it is booked, the Wola hall (option 2) is the backup. For that hall, confirm the Sunday rate, that the rental can start at 16:00 (the listing says "from evening to morning"), whether own food is allowed, the house number, access and parking.
- Child-friendliness is not described by any of the source pages. Ask the venue whether it has a quiet corner for children and whether it is safe for them (sockets, stairs, bar area).
- The EUR price depends on the exchange rate on the payment date. The budget should keep a small buffer for rate changes.
- Alterpose Studio prices come from Venuu, which no longer takes bookings in Poland. They may be out of date.

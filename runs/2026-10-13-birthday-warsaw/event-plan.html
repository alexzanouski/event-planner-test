Guest information

You are invited to a birthday party!

When: Sunday, 25 October 2026, 16:00-22:00. Please note that clocks go back one hour on the night of Saturday to Sunday (03:00 becomes 02:00). The party starts at 16:00 winter time.

Where: Events4U No.2, a private party hall in the Praga-Poludnie district of Warsaw. We will send the exact street address and the nearest stop separately as soon as the booking is confirmed.

Getting there by public transport: Warsaw's metro, trams and buses (ZTM / Warszawski Transport Publiczny) run on Sundays. Please plan your route in the ZTM or WTP journey planner once you have the address. Night buses run after the party ends at 22:00.

Getting there by car: Warsaw's paid street parking zone (SPPN) only charges Monday to Friday, 08:00-20:00, so street parking within the zone is free on Sunday. We will confirm parking at the hall itself with the address.

Programme: welcome drinks from 16:00, group photo around 16:30, buffet dinner from 16:45 (vegetarian dishes included), a children's programme with an animator from 17:45 to 19:45, birthday cake around 19:45, then a quiz and dancing. A photographer will be there all evening, 16:00-22:00.

Dress code: smart casual and comfortable shoes for dancing. The party is indoors, but it will be cool and dry outside, about 11 °C at arrival and 8-9 °C when you leave, so please bring a warm coat. Children should wear comfortable clothes they can move and play in.

What to bring: a warm coat and a good mood. Please tell us by 19 October 2026 whether you are coming, the number and ages of any children, any food allergies or dietary needs, and whether you agree to photos of your children appearing in the shared online gallery.

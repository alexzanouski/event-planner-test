## Summary
Catering comes from Restauracja Dworek's own kitchen, because the venue offers in-house catering only. The plan is a buffet dinner for all 40 guests, who sit at tables in the restaurant hall: "Bufet 2" plus the "Bufet wegetariański", with the "Napoje zimne oraz gorące bez ograniczeń" package of unlimited hot and cold soft drinks from 18:00 to 23:00. No dessert is ordered; only coffee and tea are served in the 21:00-22:30 slot. The total is 6072.00 PLN including the 10% service charge, which is 1386.11 EUR (34.65 EUR per person).

### Options considered (all Restauracja Dworek, 40 guests, incl. 10% service)

| # | Option | Per person (PLN) | Total (PLN) | Total (EUR) | Share of 2500 EUR budget | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bufet 2 (47.00) + Bufet wegetariański (45.00) + unlimited hot/cold drinks (46.00) | 151.80 | 6072.00 | 1386.11 | 55% | Selected |
| 2 | Option 1 + Bufet słodkości (41.00) | 196.90 | 7876.00 | 1797.93 | 72% | Rejected: leaves about 700 EUR for sound and decor, and the sweets are not vegan |
| 3 | Plated Zestaw 1 for 33 guests and Zestaw wegetariański/wegański for 7 (both 195.00) + drinks (46.00) | 265.10 | 10604.00 | 2420.67 | 97% | Rejected: leaves no room for sound and decor |

Selected caterer: Restauracja Dworek (in-house kitchen of the venue), option 1. It is the only caterer allowed at the venue. The buffet keeps catering at about 55% of the budget, so sound and decor still have room. Ordering the vegetarian buffet for all 40 guests, not only for 7, makes sure the meat-free dishes do not run out before the vegetarian and vegan guests have eaten.

Dessert decision: no dessert is ordered. In the program's "dessert and coffee" slot (21:00-22:30) only coffee and tea from the drinks package are served. The buffet stays out until 22:30, so guests can keep eating.

## Open issues
- Organiser task: confirm with the restaurant (+48 798 641 805, kontakt@restauracjadworek.com) that food and drink service runs until 23:00 on 2026-11-14. The published Saturday hours end at 22:00, and longer hours are agreed individually.
- Vegan details must be confirmed with the restaurant when the menu is fixed, at the latest by 2026-11-07: tortillas, focaccia and bread without dairy or egg, goat cheese served on the side of the lentil salad, a vegan dip in place of tzatziki for the vegan plates, and plant milk for coffee and tea. The offer allows dish swaps ("Możliwa jest wymiana pozycji").
- No dessert is ordered. If the user wants one later, the options are Bufet słodkości (41.00 PLN per person, 411.82 EUR for 40 incl. service, not vegan) or a cake bought by the organiser (fee 7.00 PLN per person: 280.00 PLN = 63.92 EUR for 40, or 70.31 EUR if the 10% service is also charged on it, plus the cake itself). Either option would need a budget re-check.
- The buffet is cold food. Because of the cold evening, ask the restaurant whether a hot dish (for example a soup from the set menus) can be swapped in. The offer has no price for a single dish, so this is not costed.
- The vegetarian/vegan plated set is not fully vegan: halloumi, Greek cheese, Grana Padano, Balkan cheese and possibly pesto are not vegan. It was not selected.
- Alcohol is not included and was not requested. Bringing own alcohol costs 50.00 PLN corkage per opened 0.7 l bottle; other drinks are charged at menu prices.
- The offer does not say whether the buffets can be ordered without a plated set. The restaurant has to confirm the buffet-only order when booking.
- It is not stated whether the 500.00 PLN deposit is deducted from the final bill.

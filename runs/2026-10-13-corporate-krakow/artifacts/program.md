## Summary
The corporate team party runs 18:00-23:00 on 2026-11-14 at Restauracja Dworek (ul. Papiernicza 2, Krakow). Because outdoor is preferred, guests are welcomed outside in the manor park for 20 minutes, then the rest of the evening is in the indoor restaurant hall: dinner, a team quiz, a team thank-you round, then music and socialising with a coffee and tea round. Climatology for the date gives a feels-like temperature of about 2.5-3.0 °C (below 0 °C in 2024), so the outdoor part is kept short and moves into the hall if it rains, is very windy or is too cold. Every activity is run by the organiser with the sound system planned by the services planner, so the program adds no paid items.

- Catering planner: the dinner slot is 18:45-19:45, the coffee and tea round falls in 21:00-22:30 and the buffet stays out until 22:30. A welcome drink at 18:00 (outdoors or indoors) is optional and belongs to the catering plan.

## Summary
Recommended venue: Restauracja Dworek at the Dworek Bialopradnicki manor (ul. Papiernicza 2, Krakow). It has an indoor restaurant hall with 48 seats, booked exclusively for groups of 35 or more, and a park next to the manor for an optional outdoor part. The indoor hall is the rain fallback. There is no published hall rental fee for exclusive use by 40 guests. Food is ordered from the restaurant's own per-person banquet menus, which include a vegetarian/vegan set. The 23:00 end time has to be agreed with the restaurant (see Open issues).

Notes on the options:

- Option 1: per the offer PDF, "Jako restauracja posiadamy jedna sale". The manor's halls (II and III Piwnica, next to the restaurant hall, and Sala Kominkowa and Zlota upstairs) can be used "po wczesniejszym ich wynajeciu" through the cultural centre. The cultural centre lists room hire for "bankiety ... przyjecia i imprezy okolicznosciowe" and outdoor events, with a mobile stage and technical equipment. Its price list is a PDF download on dworek.eu/wynajem and has not been checked.
- Option 1: the restaurant lists a "ZESTAW WEGETARIANSKI/ WEGANSKI: 195.00 ZL / OSOBA". Catering choices belong to the catering planner.
- Option 2: PURO says the garden has "electric heaters" so it can be used in cooler months. This is the best fit for "outdoor if possible", but it has no published price, so the budget cannot be checked yet.
- Option 3: indoor only. It has sound systems, lighting, microphones and LED screens on site, but no outdoor area.

Recommended venue: Restauracja Dworek (Centrum Kultury Dworek Bialopradnicki). The hall seats 48, which covers the 40 guests. With 40 guests the booking passes the 35-person minimum for exclusive use, so there is no published rental fee and the 2500 EUR budget stays mostly free for food, sound and decor. The manor has an indoor hall and an adjacent park, so an outdoor part is possible with an indoor fallback. Accessibility is documented (stair platforms, accessible toilets, a disabled parking space), and the venue offers a vegetarian/vegan banquet set. PURO has a heated garden, but it has no published price. COCO Club is indoor only.

Fallback space: the indoor Restauracja Dworek hall (48 seats) at ul. Papiernicza 2. Any outdoor part in the manor park moves into this hall if it rains. The park has no published covered or heated area.

## Open issues
- End time: the offer PDF says "Standardowo czas trwania przyjecia wyznaczaja godziny otwarcia restauracji". The PDF lists 12:00-21:00 and the homepage lists Saturday 12:00-22:00. For dancing-type events the time frame is "ustalane sa indywidualnie". The 18:00-23:00 window on 2026-11-14 (a Saturday) must be confirmed with the restaurant (+48 798 641 805, kontakt@restauracjadworek.com). The program planner should not plan past 22:00 until this is confirmed.
- Availability on 2026-11-14 has not been checked. The restaurant does not accept preliminary reservations ("Nie przyjmujemy rezerwacji wstepnych"). A booking needs a 500 zl deposit, and the sources do not say whether it is deducted from the final bill.
- Catering is in-house. The catering planner should use Restauracja Dworek's menus and include the 10% service charge. The offer also lists a corkage fee of 50.00 zl per opened 0.7 l bottle of the guests' own alcohol and 7.00 zl per person for an outside cake.
- The manor park has no published covered or heated area. Any outdoor part (for example a short welcome outside) depends on the weather, and its rain fallback is the indoor hall.
- The services planner should check with the cultural centre whether its mobile stage and technical equipment (dworek.eu/wynajem) can be hired for sound. That would be a separate rental with its own price list.
- Option 2 (PURO, heated garden) fits the outdoor preference best but has no published price. It can only replace the recommendation after a written quote.

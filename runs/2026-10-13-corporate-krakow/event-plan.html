Subject: Team party on Saturday, 14 November - practical information

Dear colleagues,

Thank you for confirming. We are looking forward to celebrating the year with you. Here is everything you need for the evening.

When: Saturday, 2026-11-14, 18:00-23:00. Please arrive on time, because the evening begins at 18:00 with a short welcome outside the restaurant.

Where: Restauracja Dworek, Dworek Białoprądnicki manor, ul. Papiernicza 2, 31-221 Kraków.

Getting there by public transport:

  • Tram lines 5, 7 or 18 to the stop Białoprądnicka. Line 18 runs via Teatr Bagatela and Stary Kleparz. From the main railway station, take tram 7 from the stop Dworzec Główny Tunel to Białoprądnicka.
  • Bus lines 138, 169 or 172 to the stop Mackiewicza, then a 5-minute walk to the manor. From the main railway station, take bus 105, 132 or 405 from Dworzec Główny Zachód to Opolska Estakada, then change to bus 137, 138, 169 or 172 to Mackiewicza (or walk about 22 minutes from Opolska Estakada).
  • From the Krowodrza Górka P+R interchange it is a 13-minute walk.
  • Lines and evening timetables change, so please check your route back after 23:00 in the MPK Kraków timetable or a journey planner before you leave.

By car: The car park at the manor is for staff only. The nearest public car park is in front of Zajazd Kościuszkowski, ul. Białoprądnicka 3. There is one parking space for guests with disabilities on the grounds. If you need it, please let us know in advance. Bike racks are behind the main building. As this is an evening party, we recommend public transport or a taxi.

Accessibility: The main entrance has stair platforms and automatic doors, and there are accessible toilets. Please tell us in advance if you need any help.

Programme: a welcome outside, opening words from management, a buffet dinner, a team quiz, our thank-you round with the quiz winners, a group photo, then music and time together until 23:00.

Dress code: smart casual. Mid-November evenings in Kraków are cold (around 7 °C, feeling like about 3 °C, possibly close to freezing). Please bring a warm coat, and a scarf and gloves if you like, for the 20-minute welcome outside. If the weather is bad, the welcome will be held indoors. The rest of the evening is indoors and there is a cloakroom.

Food and drinks: a buffet dinner with plenty of vegetarian and vegan dishes, plus unlimited soft drinks, coffee and tea all evening. If your dietary needs have changed since you replied, please let us know by 2026-11-07.

What to bring: just yourself, a warm coat and good team spirit for the quiz. Pens and answer sheets will be on the tables.

See you on the 14th!

The organising team

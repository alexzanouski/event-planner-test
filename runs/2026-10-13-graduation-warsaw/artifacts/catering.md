## Summary

Catering is done by the in-house kitchen of Hotel Venecia Palace. The menu and serving plan follow the venue's published package: 2 served dishes, 4 buffet dishes including 1 soup, cold appetizers, a sweet buffet, a coffee buffet and unlimited soft drinks. That package is already counted as the venue cost, so no second catering cost is added here. The published package lists only soft drinks, not alcohol, so the only extra cost line is alcohol: 100 PLN per guest (the top of the published 50-100 PLN range), 2739.35 EUR for 120 guests. The 330 PLN rate is a wedding price, so the venue has to quote a corporate price for this event.

## Details

### Selected caterer

Selected caterer: Hotel Venecia Palace in-house catering (ul. Kasztanowa 44, 05-816 Michałowice).

Why:
- It is the recommended venue, and its published package covers food, soft drinks and service by waiters and a coordinator. Using the in-house kitchen avoids a second vendor and a second catering bill.
- The venue's published package is "od 330 zł/os." for "od 30 osób (piątek, sobota, niedziela)". 2026-11-28 is a Saturday and 120 guests is above the minimum. This is a **wedding** package. The corporate price for a graduation party on this date is not published, so it must be quoted by the venue (see Open issues).
- The venue's kitchen has served vegetarian dishes before. Its 2024/2025 New Year's Eve menu has a "VEGE" item, "Cotoletta z bakłażana" (aubergine cutlet), and several vegetarian cold dishes and salads.

### What the published package includes (from the venue's wedding page)

- "2 dania serwowane + 4 dania w bufetach (w tym 1 zupa)" (2 served dishes and 4 buffet dishes, including 1 soup)
- "Przystawki zimne (mięsa pieczone, ryby, wędliny, sałatki)" (cold appetizers: roast meats, fish, cold cuts, salads)
- Sweet buffet ("bufet słodki"). The package table on the same page lists a served dessert ("deser serw.") only for the higher tiers, so a served dessert is not planned here.
- "Bufet kawowy (kawa, herbata, ciasta)" (coffee buffet: coffee, tea, cakes)
- "Napoje bez limitu (soki, woda, napoje gazowane Coca cola, fanta, sprite)" (unlimited drinks: juices, water, Coca-Cola, Fanta, Sprite)
- A welcome "kieliszek wina musującego" (glass of sparkling wine). The wedding page lists this as part of the couple's welcome. Whether it is part of a corporate package has to be confirmed.

### Alcohol: what the sources say

- The venue's wedding page says the "unlimited drinks" are juices, water and soft drinks only. It does not mention alcohol, a drinks package or a corkage fee. Alcohol is therefore **not** treated as included.
- The venue's New Year's Eve 2024/2025 listing (pik.warszawa.pl) says "ALKOHOL – bez ograniczeń" (vodka Finlandia, wine, beer) and that guests "można wnieść swój własny ulubiony alkohol" (may bring their own favourite alcohol). This shows the venue has allowed guests to bring their own alcohol, but only for that New Year's Eve event.
- Plan: the organiser buys the alcohol (wine, beer, sparkling wine and optional vodka) and the venue staff serve it. A published 2026 cost guide for Polish events puts alcohol at "50-100 zł za osobę" (50-100 PLN per guest). This plan budgets the upper end, 100 PLN per guest, to stay on the safe side.
- Rough quantities, using a published Polish guide: one 0.75 l bottle of wine per 3-4 guests (30-40 bottles for 120), about 0.1 l of sparkling wine per guest for a toast (about 17-18 bottles at 7 glasses per bottle), and 1.5-2 beers per adult guest (180-240 bottles). The guide also suggests putting 10-15% of the alcohol budget into alcohol-free options.

## Open issues

- The 330 PLN per person package is a wedding package. The venue has to quote a corporate price for a graduation party for 120 guests on Saturday 2026-11-28, with the same menu structure. The venue cost (owned by the venue planner) depends on this quote.
- Service hours: the venue's restaurant page gives restaurant hours of 14:00-21:00. Banquet service until 23:00 (sweet and coffee buffets until 22:45, bar until 23:00) must be confirmed in writing with the venue.
- Dessert: the venue's wedding page is not consistent. One list says "Deser serwowany + bufet deserowy", but the package table lists a served dessert ("deser serw.") only for the higher tiers and "bufet słodki" for the base tier. This plan uses only the sweet buffet. Ask the venue whether a served dessert is included in the corporate quote.
- Alcohol: the published package lists only soft drinks. Confirm in writing whether the venue allows organiser-supplied alcohol for this event (the only evidence is the NYE 2024/2025 listing), whether there is a corkage or service fee, and whether the venue offers its own drinks package instead. If it does, its price replaces the 100 PLN per guest line.
- Confirm that the welcome glass of sparkling wine, listed as part of the wedding package, is also included for a corporate event.
- The named dishes are examples from the venue's NYE 2024/2025 menu. The NYE hot buffet had no meat-free hot dish (the aubergine casserole contains meat). The final menu must include a vegetarian served main ("Cotoletta z bakłażana" or an equal dish), a vegetarian hot buffet dish and preferably a vegetarian soup, agreed with the venue kitchen.
- The number of vegetarian served mains is collected with the RSVPs and passed to the venue by a deadline agreed with the venue.
- Confirm that the venue has hot tea and coffee ready at 21:40, when guests come back in from the fireworks.
- Alcohol quantities depend on how much the guests drink. The 100 PLN per guest is a budget cap, not a fixed price. The actual spend can be lower.

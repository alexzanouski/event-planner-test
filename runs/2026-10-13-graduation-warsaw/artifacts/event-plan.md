### Guest information

> **Graduation party - AI course by Godel Technologies**
>
> Congratulations on finishing the course! We are celebrating together on **Saturday, 28 November 2026, 17:00-23:00**.
>
> **Where:** Hotel Venecia Palace, ul. Kasztanowa 44, 05-816 Michałowice (just outside Warsaw).
>
> **Programme:** arrival, registration and welcome drink from 17:00, official opening at 17:45, speeches and the certificate ceremony, dinner at 19:15, awards and dancing. At about 21:15 we put our coats on and go out to the palace garden for a short fireworks show of about 3 minutes at 21:30, and we are back inside for hot drinks by about 21:40. The evening ends at 23:00.
>
> **By car:** about 15 minutes from the Palace of Culture and Science in central Warsaw. The hotel has its own supervised car park, free of charge, no reservation needed.
>
> **By public transport:** take the WKD suburban train from Warszawa Śródmieście WKD (or Warszawa Ochota WKD / Warszawa Zachodnia WKD) to the station **Michałowice**. From there continue by local GPA bus **61** (Reguły - Michałowice - Michałowice-Wieś - Opacz; on Saturdays about every 30 minutes, service until about 23:00) or by taxi. Please check your connection in the WKD and GPA journey planners before you go. The last buses run around the end of the party, so plan a taxi for the way home.
>
> **Please do not drink and drive.** Alcohol will be served. Share a ride with a sober driver or take a taxi home.
>
> **Dress code:** smart / cocktail. The fireworks are outdoors and the evening will be cold, around 0 °C (it can be as low as -5 °C), so bring a warm coat, a hat and gloves, and wear shoes you can walk in on the garden paths. There is a coat check at the venue.
>
> **What to bring:** a good mood, your warm coat for the fireworks, and your phone for photos. Certificates will be handed out on the evening, so you do not need to bring anything for the ceremony. A professional photographer will be there.
>
> **Food:** dinner and drinks are included. If you have not told us yet whether you want the vegetarian main course, reply by 10 November 2026.
>
> If the weather is too windy or wet for fireworks, we stay inside and have a short spark or light show in the main hall at the same time instead.
>
> See you there!

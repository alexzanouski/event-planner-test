## Summary

A graduation evening for the Godel Technologies AI course at Hotel Venecia Palace, Michałowice, on 2026-11-28, from 17:00 to 23:00 (CET), with 120 adult guests. The program runs indoors in the palace halls. It goes welcome, speeches, certificate ceremony, dinner, awards and dancing. The only outdoor part is the fireworks block in the palace garden from 21:30 to 21:40, the calmest part of the evening in the weather data. Within it the show itself lasts about 3 minutes. Guests go out in coats and come back to hot drinks. If the weather makes the fireworks unsafe, a short indoor cold-spark or light show in the main hall takes their place within the same block.

### Spaces used

- Main hall at Hotel Venecia Palace: stage area for the opening, speeches, certificates and awards; tables for dinner; dance floor for the party. The venue says its halls can be freely arranged and hold events of up to 300 people, so one hall layout with a stage, round tables and a dance floor fits 120 guests.
- Foyer and coat check: arrival, welcome drink, and coats before and after the garden slot.
- Palace garden: fireworks only, 21:15-21:40 including the walk out. A fireworks show in this garden is documented in the venue's 2024/2025 New Year's Eve listing.

### Weather-driven choices

- The fireworks block is placed at 21:30-21:40. In the reference data, wind and gusts drop through the evening (mean gusts 20.5 km/h at 21:00 and 19.4 km/h at 22:00, against 22.4-23.2 km/h at 17:00-18:00), and no rain was recorded at 20:00-23:00 in any of the three years.
- Guests spend about 25 minutes outdoors in total (21:15-21:40), with coats, because it will be around 0 °C. The show itself is short, about 3 minutes. Hot drinks wait for guests when they come back.

## Rain fallback

Decision:
- At 12:00 on the event day the organiser and the licensed fireworks provider check the latest forecast.
- At 21:00 the provider makes the final go or no-go call on site. The provider's own safety limits for wind, gusts and precipitation apply (the weather outlook does not set them).
- The host announces the result at 21:15.

If the fireworks are cancelled (rain, snow, strong wind or gusts above the provider's limit):
- Guests stay inside and keep their coats in the coat check. Nobody goes into the garden.
- 21:15-21:30: the dance set continues in the main hall. The DJ or band clears the front of the dance floor, and the provider or the DJ's technician sets up the indoor effect.
- 21:30-21:40: guests gather around the dance floor for a short indoor highlight in the main hall. This is either a cold-spark show (cold-spark fountains placed at the front of the dance floor) or a light show. It is the alternative the client accepted in the requirements. Applause and photos follow, and the photographer covers it from the dance floor.
- 21:40-21:55: no coat return is needed. Hot drinks are served in the hall as planned, and the dancing goes on.
- From 21:55 the program continues unchanged until 23:00.
- The end time and all other slots stay the same, so the program still fits 17:00-23:00.

Conditions:
- Cold sparks indoors need the venue's permission, which is not yet confirmed. The venue must check its fire alarm and smoke detectors and give the minimum distance to guests and ceiling.
- If the venue does not allow cold sparks in the main hall, the fallback is a light show only, run by the DJ or band's lighting with no pyrotechnics.
- The indoor effect is ordered by the services planner, either as an add-on from the fireworks provider or from the DJ/band, so there is no second vendor and no cost in this program.

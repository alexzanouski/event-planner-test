## Constraints

- Budget: total must not exceed 18000 EUR (150 EUR per person for 120 guests).
- Guest count: venue capacity must cover 120 guests.
- Time window: the program must fit into 17:00-23:00 on 2026-11-28.
- Indoor/outdoor: main event indoors; fireworks show outdoors at the venue, so the venue must allow fireworks.
- Rain fallback: if rain or strong wind makes the outdoor fireworks unsafe, an indoor alternative (e.g. cold sparklers or a light show) replaces it.
- Fireworks: must be performed by a licensed professional provider.
- Dietary restrictions: none. The menu should still include vegetarian options as a wish, which is not checked as a restriction.

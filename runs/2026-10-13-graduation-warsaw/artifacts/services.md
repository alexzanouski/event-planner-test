## Summary

Four services are planned for Hotel Venecia Palace on 2026-11-28: DJ Adam Pokora for music (off-season sound and lighting rate of 3000 PLN, up to 8 hours), the event photographer ZDJECIAEVENTOWE.PL (6-hour package for 1950 PLN gross with 150 edited photos), and a 3-minute professional fireworks show by PyroTech SHOW in the palace garden (Bronze package, 2337 PLN gross per minute, plus travel). PyroTech says it holds the licences, permits and certificates required by state authorities and has an insurance policy. If wind or rain makes the fireworks unsafe, the indoor alternative is two cold-spark machines from FrameRent (Warsaw), which the vendor says are safe for indoor use. All services together are budgeted at 3018.12 EUR.

### Licences and insurance of the fireworks provider (what the source says)

- PyroTech company page: "PyroTech posiada wszelkie wymagane przez organy państwowe koncesje, pozwolenia i certyfikaty" (PyroTech holds all licences, permits and certificates required by state authorities). It describes the team as qualified pyrotechnicians and says the company handles the show notifications and approvals.
- PyroTech home page: "Posiadamy wszelkie wymagane certyfikaty, szkolenia, pozwolenia" (we hold all required certificates, training and permits) and mentions a "polisę ubezpieczeniową do realizacji weselnych pokazów fajerwerków" (an insurance policy for wedding fireworks shows).
- The pages do not give a licence number, the insurer or the sum insured. The insurance is described for wedding shows, so cover for a corporate event has to be confirmed in writing (see Open issues).

### Same provider for the indoor alternative

PyroTech also offers "zimne ognie na wesele" (hand-held cold sparklers) and stage effects ("iskry sceniczne", "wodospady iskier"). Its pages say hand-held cold sparklers can be lit in a hall if the venue allows it. But PyroTech publishes no prices for these ("nie korzystamy z gotowych cenników", we do not use ready-made price lists). For that reason the priced FrameRent machines are the planned fallback. PyroTech can be asked to quote its own stage sparks, and its quote would replace the FrameRent line if it is accepted (see Open issues).

### Fit with the venue

- Space: the venue's halls hold up to 300 people and can be freely arranged, so a DJ booth, a dance floor and two spark machines fit for 120 guests.
- Power: the DJ system is more than 3000 W RMS. Each spark machine needs "220-240 V, moc 600 W" (1200 W for two). The venue does not publish its power supply, so the DJ and FrameRent need separate standard sockets near the dance floor (see Open issues).
- Sound limits: the venue publishes no sound limit. It hosts New Year's Eve parties with dancing, so a DJ is normal there. Silent fireworks (SILENTY®) are an option if the venue or neighbours ask for less noise.
- Fireworks location: the palace garden, which a 2024/2025 event listing shows was used for a fireworks show (see venue research).

### Fit with the weather

- Climatology for 2026-11-28 (three reference years, not a forecast): almost dry from 17:00 to 23:00, with wind of at most 13.3 km/h and gusts of at most 23.8 km/h. Wind is lowest around 21:00-22:00, which is why the fireworks block (21:30-21:40) falls in that period. A 3-minute show keeps guests outside only briefly in about 0 °C.
- The weather check gives no wind threshold. PyroTech decides whether to go ahead against the real forecast, which is available from 2026-11-12. If it calls off the show, the FrameRent machines are used in the main hall at the same time.
- All other services are indoors and do not depend on the weather.

## Open issues

- Fireworks licence and insurance: PyroTech states in general terms that it holds the required licences, permits and certificates and has an insurance policy. Before booking, ask for the lead pyrotechnician's licence (uprawnienia pirotechniczne), an insurance certificate that covers a corporate event at Hotel Venecia Palace, and confirmation that PyroTech files the show notification for Gmina Michałowice.
- Fireworks prices are published for weddings. Confirm that the same per-minute prices apply to a corporate event, ask for the cancellation terms if the show is called off for wind or rain, and ask about the go/no-go wind threshold. The travel cost uses 134 km counted one way. The 23% VAT on travel was added by this plan and has to be confirmed.
- The venue has to confirm in writing that a fireworks show is allowed in the palace garden on 2026-11-28 and that cold-spark machines are allowed in the main hall (see venue research).
- DJ: the price list says "+ dojazd" and only prices travel inside Warsaw. The travel cost to Michałowice is not published and has to be quoted. The page does not say whether the 3000 PLN is net or gross, or whether it applies to corporate events. Also confirm the DJ is free on 2026-11-28. If the hosting should be in English (for example for international course participants), add 500 PLN (114.14 EUR).
- Photographer: the page does not mention travel outside Warsaw, Saturday or evening surcharges, or image usage rights for company marketing. Confirm these and the date.
- FrameRent: the page does not say whether 490 PLN is net or gross, whether the price is for one machine, or whether the spark granulate, delivery to Michałowice and an operator are included. Get a quote for 2 machines including granulate and delivery. Alternatively, ask PyroTech to quote its own stage sparks so that one provider covers both the show and the fallback.
- Power: the DJ (more than 3000 W RMS) and two spark machines (600 W each) need separate sockets near the dance floor. Confirm the available power with the venue.
- None of the vendors publishes a calendar, so availability on 2026-11-28 has to be confirmed by each vendor.

## Summary

Recommended venue: Hotel Venecia Palace in Michałowice (Pruszków county, outside the Warsaw city limits, about 15 minutes by car from the city centre). Its own site says it hosts integration events for up to 300 people in freely arrangeable halls, its published package price starts at 330 PLN per person (about 75.33 EUR), and a published event listing for the venue describes a fireworks show in the palace garden. Pałac w Jabłonnie and Gościniec Maria are shortlisted as alternatives.

### Fireworks permission (what the sources say)

- Hotel Venecia Palace: a Warsaw city events listing (pik.warszawa.pl) for the venue's New Year's Eve 2024/2025 party says: "Symbolicznym zwieńczeniem imprezy jest fenomenalny pokaz fajerwerków w ogrodzie pałacowym" (the culmination of the event is a fireworks show in the palace garden). The venue's own wedding page lists "Salut armatni odpalany przez Młodą Parę" (a cannon salute fired by the couple) as an included feature. The venue's own pages do not state a general fireworks policy for private or corporate events.
- Pałac w Jabłonnie: a city events listing for the venue's New Year's Eve 2021/2022 ball says: "Nowy Rok powitamy wspólnym toastem i pokazem sztucznych ogni" (we will welcome the New Year with a toast and a fireworks show). The venue's own wedding page does not mention fireworks.
- Gościniec Maria: no source mentions fireworks. The page mentions grilling and bonfires on the grounds only.

### Open until 23:00 (what the sources say)

- Hotel Venecia Palace: no closing time is published. The venue hosts New Year's Eve parties with dancing and a garden fireworks show (pik.warszawa.pl listing), which means events run past 23:00, but this has to be confirmed in writing for 2026-11-28.
- Pałac w Jabłonnie: the venue's wedding page says "the dance party is organised until dawn".
- Gościniec Maria: no end time is published.

### Comparison

- Capacity for 120 guests (G2): Venecia Palace (up to 300) and Gościniec Maria (up to 200 at tables) have room for 120 guests plus a dance floor and DJ setup. Pałac w Jabłonnie gives exactly 120 as its maximum, so there is no spare space for the dance floor, DJ and photographer.
- Price: only Venecia Palace publishes a price. At 330 PLN per person, 120 guests cost 39600 PLN = 9039.86 EUR, which leaves 8960.14 EUR of the 18000 EUR budget for DJ, photographer and fireworks.
- Fireworks: Venecia Palace has the most recent evidence (fireworks in the palace garden, 2024/2025). Jabłonna's evidence is older (2021/2022). There is no evidence for Gościniec Maria.

Recommended venue: Hotel Venecia Palace. It is in the suburbs outside Warsaw, its halls hold up to 300 people, so 120 guests plus a dance floor and DJ fit with room to spare. It is the only option with a published price, and that price fits the budget. A 2024/2025 event listing describes a fireworks show in its own palace garden. It also has free on-site parking, accessible rooms and wheelchairs.

Fallback space: the indoor halls of Hotel Venecia Palace. The whole program runs indoors anyway. If rain or strong wind makes the outdoor fireworks unsafe, the indoor alternative accepted in the requirements (cold sparklers or a light show) takes place in the main hall. Whether cold sparklers are allowed indoors has to be confirmed with the venue (see Open issues).

## Open issues

- Fireworks permission is not stated as a policy on the venue's own pages. The evidence for Hotel Venecia Palace is a 2024/2025 New Year's Eve listing on pik.warszawa.pl describing a garden fireworks show. Before booking, get written confirmation that the venue allows a show by a licensed provider in the palace garden on 2026-11-28, and that the provider's insurance and any required notification to the municipality (Gmina Michałowice) are handled.
- The 330 PLN per person price comes from the venue's wedding page and is a starting price ("od"). The corporate price for this date has to be confirmed with the venue. The New Year's Eve price was 600-700 PLN per person; if the venue quotes in that range (72000-84000 PLN = 16436.10-19175.46 EUR), the 18000 EUR budget would not be enough.
- The package price includes food and beverages ("multiple courses, buffets and beverages"). The catering planner should treat the venue's in-house catering as the caterer and must not add a second full catering cost on top. Vegetarian options (a wish) have to be confirmed with the venue kitchen.
- A 23:00 end time is not published. The venue runs New Year's Eve parties, so late events are possible, but the 17:00-23:00 slot has to be confirmed in writing.
- Rain/wind fallback: confirm that cold sparklers or a light show are allowed indoors in the main hall.
- Pałac w Jabłonnie has a maximum of exactly 120 guests and no published price. It is only a backup option.
- Gościniec Maria has no published price, end time or fireworks policy. It is only a backup option.
- No weather claims are made here. The fireworks decision depends on the forecast.
